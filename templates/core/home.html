01 — About

WHO I AM

I'm Shem a Django engineer who designs and ships full-stack web applications — from database schema and REST APIs to polished, responsive front-ends. I care about clean architecture, fast load times, and code that the next developer can actually read.

Over the years I've delivered multiple production projects for trusted, repeat customers — SaaS dashboards, e-commerce platforms, internal tools, and content-driven sites. Every project I take on gets the same treatment: clear scoping, weekly check-ins, and a handover that leaves the client in full control of their product.

I work end-to-end. That means I can take an idea from a rough sketch to a deployed Django app with authentication, an admin panel, a REST API, and a front-end that feels considered — not templated.
